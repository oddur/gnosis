---
title: Notifications & menu bar
description: Get told when a PR needs you, and see your queue from the menu bar.
order: 12
---

Gnosis sends a notification when something needs you: a review request, a mention, a reply, requested changes, an approval, failed checks, a PR that is ready to merge, or a finished explanation. Choose which events notify you, and pause them when you need to focus. On macOS, the menu bar icon shows how many PRs need you. Click it to see a summary panel or a classic menu. Set this with <Setting name="Clicking the icon shows" section="Notifications" /> and <Setting name="Number on the icon" section="Notifications" />.
