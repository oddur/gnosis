Gnosis tells you when a PR needs you. It sends a desktop notification when Gnosis is in the background, and an in-app notification when Gnosis is in front. On macOS, the menu bar icon also shows what needs you.

## When to use notifications and the menu bar

- Use **notifications** to know at once when someone asks for your review, mentions you, or replies to you.
- Use the **menu bar** to see what needs you without opening the Gnosis window.
- **Pause** notifications when you must focus.

## Notifications

Select a notification to open the PR at the right place. On macOS, you can reply to a mention, a reply or a change request from the notification itself. The reply posts to that thread on GitHub. On other systems, the notification opens the thread. Turn this on or off with <Setting name="Allow replies from notifications" section="Notifications" />.

### Choose the events

Open Settings › Notifications. Turn each event on or off:

- **Reviews you owe**: review requested from you, review requested from your team, new commits since your review, mentions, and replies to you.
- **Your PRs**: changes requested, approved, checks failed, and ready to merge.
- **Snoozed PRs**: a snoozed PR is back.
- **Gnosis**: explanation ready, and update ready.

Review requests from your team are off by default. Teams such as CODEOWNERS can request you often.

Turn the two kinds of notification on or off with <Setting name="Desktop notifications" section="Notifications" /> and <Setting name="In-app notifications" section="Notifications" />.

### Choose the repositories

Watched repositories notify you about all events, unless you set an exception for them. For other repositories, set <Setting name="Other repositories" section="Notifications" /> to **All**, **Only when it's on me** or **None**. To set a different level for one repository, add it under <Setting name="Repository exceptions" section="Notifications" />. You can also set the level from a PR's menu.

### Bots

Bots never notify you. To hear from a bot, add its login under <Setting name="Bots" section="Notifications" />.

### Pause notifications

In the **Quiet** section, choose **Pause for 1 hour**, **Until tomorrow morning** or **Until Monday**. Select **Resume** to end the pause early. While notifications are paused, Gnosis still marks PRs unread. When the pause ends, you get one summary.

Gnosis also follows your system's Focus and Do Not Disturb settings.

## The menu bar

On macOS, Gnosis has an icon in the menu bar. The icon can show a number of PRs that need you.

<DocShot
  name="menubar-panel"
  alt="The Gnosis menu bar panel. It lists PRs in three groups: To review, To fix and Ready. An Open Gnosis button is at the bottom."
  caption="The menu bar panel."
  title="Menu bar"
/>

Click the icon to open the panel. The panel groups the PRs into **To review**, **To fix** and **Ready**. Select a PR to open it in Gnosis. Use the arrow keys, or <Kbd keys="j" /> and <Kbd keys="k" />, to move through the list. The panel never writes to GitHub.

When the icon shows the summary panel, right-click it for **Open Gnosis**, **Settings…** and **Quit Gnosis**. When it shows the classic menu, right-click also opens the classic menu.

### Set up the menu bar

In Settings › Notifications, under **Menu bar**:

- <Setting name="Clicking the icon shows" section="Notifications" />: **Summary panel** or **Classic menu**.
- <Setting name="Number on the icon" section="Notifications" />: **New since you last looked**, **Everything that needs you**, **A dot only** or **Nothing**. The Dock icon shows the same number.

To hide the icon, turn off <Setting name="Menu bar icon" section="Integrations" />.
