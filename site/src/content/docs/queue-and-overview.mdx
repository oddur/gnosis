The Review Queue lists the open PRs that Gnosis watches for you. The Overview is a morning page above the queue. It tells you what changed, what to do next, and who waits on whom.

## When to use the Overview and the queue

- Use the **Overview** at the start of the day, or when you come back to work. It answers one question: what do I do now?
- Use the **queue** to find a PR, to work through a list of PRs, or to act on many PRs at the same time.

## The Overview

Select **Overview** at the top of the sidebar. The page starts with a briefing sentence. Each number in it is a link to the rows that it counts. A **Since yesterday** line and a freshness line follow.

The page has these sections:

- **Starred**: the PRs and stacks that you starred. This section shows only when you starred something.
- **Next up**: up to five things to do. People who wait on you come first, then approved merges, then fixes.
- **Waiting on you**: the people who wait for your review or your reply.
- **Waiting on others**: your PRs, grouped by the person or the check that blocks them.
- **Needs a decision**: your PRs that fail, have no approval, or did not change for more than a week.

Each row has one action, for example **Merge**, **Review**, **Review again**, **Reply** or **Open**. In **Waiting on others**, **Remind** asks a reviewer for a review again. In **Needs a decision**, **Close PR** closes a PR without a merge. **Remind** and **Close PR** open a confirmation before Gnosis writes to GitHub.

To move through the rows, press <Kbd keys="j" /> and <Kbd keys="k" />. Press <Kbd keys="a" /> to do the row's action, and <Kbd keys="z" /> to snooze the row.

## The Review Queue

The sidebar has the queue's lists:

- **Review queue**: all the PRs that match your filters.
- **Starred**, **Review requests**, **Needs my response**, **Updated PRs** and **Stacks**.
- **Saved reviews**: the explanations that you generated before.
- **Saved views**: the views that you saved.
- **Snoozed** and **Muted PRs**.

Select a PR to see its briefing beside the list. Press <Kbd keys="Enter" /> to open it. Press <Kbd keys="⌘ \" /> to show or hide the sidebar.

### Filter, sort and group

1. Open the filters from the queue toolbar.
2. Filter by read state, involvement, repository, author, target branch, draft state, dates, labels, PR or stack, explanation state, briefing state, or merge conflicts.
3. Open the sort menu and choose an order, for example **Priority**, **Last updated**, **Change size** or **Unread first**. **Starred first** keeps your starred PRs at the top.
4. Open **Group by** and choose **None**, **Action** or **Repository**.

Press <Kbd keys="/" /> to search the queue.

### Save a view

When the filters, sort and grouping show what you need, select **Save view…** and give the view a name. The view shows in the sidebar under **Saved views**.

### Star, snooze and mute

- **Star** a PR or a stack to keep it at the top of the queue and in the Overview.
- **Snooze** a PR to hide it until later. Choose **Later today**, **Tomorrow morning** or **Next week**. The PR comes back at that time.
- **Mute** a PR to hide it until you unmute it. Muted PRs are in **Muted PRs**.

These actions are in the menu of each row. Right-click the row, or press <Kbd keys="⇧ F10" />.

### Act on many PRs

Press <Kbd keys="⌘ A" /> to select every PR in the list. Press <Kbd keys="⌘ Space" /> to add one PR to the selection, or to remove it. The selection menu can mark PRs as read or unread, generate explanations or briefings, star, copy links, snooze and mute.

## The command palette

Press <Kbd keys="⌘ K" /> to open the command palette. Type to find PRs, code, explanations and commands. Start with `>` to show only commands.
