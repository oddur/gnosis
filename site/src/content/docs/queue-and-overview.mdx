title: Queue & Overview
description: See which PRs need you, then filter, sort, group, save views, star, snooze and mute them.

---

The Review Queue lists the PRs in your watched repositories. Its sidebar sections show all PRs, PRs that wait for your response, review requests, updated PRs, stacks, saved views, and starred, snoozed and muted PRs. The Overview is a morning page: a briefing sentence, what changed since yesterday, what to do next, and the PRs that wait on you or on others. Filter the queue by state, people, repository, branch, labels and more. Sort it and group it by action or repository, then save the result as a view. Select more than one PR to act on all of them. Use <Kbd keys="j" /> and <Kbd keys="k" /> to move through the queue, <Kbd keys="z" /> to snooze, and <Kbd keys="⌘ K" /> to open the command palette.
