---
title: Merging & PR actions
description: Merge, update, turn on auto-merge, and change a PR's state from the PR header.
order: 6
---

The PR header has the actions that change a PR on GitHub. You can merge it, turn auto-merge on or off, update its branch with a merge or a rebase, mark it ready for review, convert it to a draft, close or reopen it, and delete its branch. Gnosis shows whether the PR can merge, and why not. Each action opens a confirmation first; nothing merges without a click or Enter. Press <Kbd keys="⌘ ⇧ M" /> to open the merge confirmation and <Kbd keys="⌘ ⇧ U" /> to open the Update menu. Gnosis shows the result at once and tells you if GitHub rejects the change.
