---
title: Comments & reviews
description: Comment on lines, reply to and resolve threads, keep drafts, and submit a review.
order: 5
---

You can comment on a line or a range of lines in any diff. Each comment starts as a draft. The Drafts panel in the PR header shows the comments that you did not send yet. On the Summary tab, reply to a thread or resolve it. Press <Kbd keys="n" /> to go to the next unresolved thread, <Kbd keys="r" /> to reply, and <Kbd keys="e" /> to resolve. To finish, submit a review: Approve, Comment, or Request changes. Gnosis sends your drafts with the review.
