---
title: Resolving conflicts
description: Resolve merge conflicts with proposals that you check before Gnosis pushes.
order: 9
---

When a PR has merge conflicts, Gnosis sorts each conflict into one of three groups. Merged conflicts have a proven resolution. Check conflicts have a proposal that you must confirm. Decide conflicts need your choice. For each conflict, keep the PR's change, the base branch's change, or both, or edit the result. Press <Kbd keys="1" />, <Kbd keys="2" /> or <Kbd keys="3" /> to choose, and <Kbd keys="a" /> to use the proposal and go to the next conflict. Gnosis never pushes without your confirmation: <Kbd keys="⌘ ↵" /> opens the Review and push confirmation.
